## 🎯 THE 5 DEMO COMMANDMENTS

### 1. **Beat 3 is your signature — don't rush it**
When the Case Writer refuses to write the dossier, STOP TALKING for 3 full seconds. Let them read it. This is what separates you from every other team. Everyone else will show AI that produces output. You're showing AI that *refuses* to produce bad output until the evidence is right.

### 2. **Narrate OVER the stream, don't just read it**
You're not reading a script. You're interpreting what the AI is doing. "Watch — it's not just sorting by score, it's asking if this is part of something bigger." Make them understand the *thinking*, not just the output.

### 3. **The timer reveal is your closer**
When you glance at the timer and it shows ~90 seconds, pause. Point. Let them see it. "Ninety seconds. From 847 claims to a prosecutable case file." This is your mic drop.

### 4. **Stop when you're done**
After "Three agents. One click. Ninety seconds." — STOP. Don't fill silence. Don't add "so yeah" or "that's it" or "any questions?" Let it land. The judges need processing time.

### 5. **If the demo fails, don't apologize**
Say "Let me show you the recorded run from this morning" and play backup video with full confidence. Never debug on stage. Never say "sorry." Technical issues happen. Winners have backups.
